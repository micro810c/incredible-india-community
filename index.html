Trust & safety

Trust & safety — how it will work

Trust is the foundation. Here is exactly how verification and safety will work when the community opens — nothing here is live yet, and there are no verification badges in this preview.

Verification (planned)

  • Email verification for every member
  • Mobile number verification
  • Identity check before hosting or guiding
  • Separate host / guide checks before anyone can accept guests

What we will NEVER ask for publicly

  • No Aadhaar numbers in public
  • No passport numbers in public
  • No identity documents posted or shared openly
  • Documents, when needed, go only through a secure verification step — never on a profile

Report & block

Every profile and conversation will carry report and block options. Reported members are reviewed by the community team, and repeat or serious reports lead to removal.

Complaints

A clear complaint path with acknowledgement and a defined response window. Safety complaints are prioritised over everything else.

Emergency support

Members will get India emergency information in one place — police 112, women’s helpline 1091, tourist helpline 1800-11-1363 — plus guidance to contact local authorities first in any real emergency.

Meet smart

First meetings in public places, itineraries shared with family, and no advance payments outside the platform once payments launch.

Solo women travellers — how it will work

First meetings always in a public place. A women-host option and a female-host filter are planned for Phase 2, so a solo woman traveller can choose a woman host or guide. Share your itinerary with family before you travel, and keep the emergency numbers above saved in your phone. This is how it will work — none of it is a live filter or a live guarantee in this preview.

FAQ

Questions, answered honestly

Is it safe to stay with a host I met online?

Safety is why verification comes before hosting. In the live version, hosts and guides pass identity and profile checks before accepting guests, first meetings are encouraged in public places, and report/block tools are built in. In this preview, no real hosting is happening yet.

What’s the difference between hosting, companionship and a paid service?

Free Hospitality means a host welcomes you without charge, the Couchsurfing way. Local Companionship means a local spends time showing you their city, also without a service fee. Paid Service is transparent and declared upfront — for example a professional guide’s charges. Every profile will state clearly which one it is.

How do payments work?

They don’t — yet. Payments are not live in this Phase 1 preview. When membership and paid services launch, payments will run through recognised Indian and international payment providers, with receipts and cancellation terms shown before you pay.

Can you help with my India visa?

We can share general, publicly available information about India’s e-Visa process and official links, and a local friend can help you plan around your dates. We are not a visa agent and cannot apply for, guarantee or expedite any visa — always apply through the official Government of India visa website.

I’m a student. How do I earn as a guide?

When guide registration opens (after verification is live), you’ll create a guide profile with your city, languages and interests, complete the guide check, and set your availability. Visitors then request walks and experiences through the platform.

Is Incredible India a government website?

No. This is an independent community initiative by its founders. It is not affiliated with the Government of India or the Ministry of Tourism.

What if my host cancels or doesn’t show up?

In the live version, you will be able to report a no-show, and the community team will help you find an alternative host or guide. Hosts who cancel repeatedly or don’t show up lose hosting access. In this preview, no real hosting is happening yet, so there is nothing to cancel.

Can families with children stay with a host?

Yes — in the live version, families can say how many children are travelling and their ages when they request a stay, and hosts can say whether their home suits children. Safety and comfort for children come first: first meetings in public, and either side can decline a match.

Do I need to speak Hindi?

No. Hosts and guides list the languages they speak, and you can filter by language. English works in most cities and tourist areas; a local friend also helps with the Hindi you actually need — numbers, directions, food and fares.
